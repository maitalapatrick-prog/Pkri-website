🧾 PKRI Fact Check & Verification

Our Verification Principles

  • Separate allegations from established facts.
  • Preserve original evidence where possible.
  • Verify dates, sources and context.
  • Seek responses from affected parties.
  • Correct errors when credible new evidence emerges.
  • Do not publish private information unnecessarily.
